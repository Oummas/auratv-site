Frequently asked questions

How do I add a source?

On first launch, or in Settings, choose to add a source and enter the details given by your provider: Xtream credentials or an M3U playlist link. AuraTV provides no source.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple account as for the purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

The AuraTV+ subscription is managed by Apple. In your iPhone Settings, tap your name then Subscriptions, or use "Manage subscription" in the app. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Parental controls and a forgotten code

Adult content is hidden by default, everywhere in the app. The 4-digit code is required to reveal it, turn the filter off or change the code. If you forgot it, write to us.

AI and my privacy

AI is only used for natural-language search and a few helpers, and only when you use them. There is no account and no advertising tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.
