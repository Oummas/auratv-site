What AuraTV does

Natural-language search

Describe what you feel like watching and AuraTV+ suggests ideas. 7-day free trial, then 2.99 € per month or 19.99 € per year. The rest of the app stays free.

Multiview up to 9 screens

Show up to 9 streams at once, depending on your device.

Parental controls on by default

Adult content from your provider is hidden from the start. A 4-digit code lets you reveal it.

10 languages

The app is translated into ten languages.

iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

One app, designed for touch and for the remote.
